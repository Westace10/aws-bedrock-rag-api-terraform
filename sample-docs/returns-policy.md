# Example returns policy (sample document for the demo)

Customers can return most items within 30 days of purchase with a receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return being processed.

Opened software, personalised items and perishable food cannot be returned unless faulty.

Faulty items can be returned within 12 months. If a repair is not possible, the customer is offered a replacement or a full refund.
